About Birsa Institute of Technology

Our Legacy and History

Established in **1956**, Birsa Institute of Technology has a long and proud history as one of the premier technical institutions in the region. Founded with a vision to catalyze industrial growth, the institute quickly became a beacon for engineering education, providing skilled human resources to the nation's core sectors. For nearly seven decades, we have continuously evolved, expanding our academic offerings and embracing cutting-edge research while maintaining our foundational commitment to excellence and public service.

Our alumni network spans the globe, holding leadership positions in major corporations, government bodies, and academia, a testament to the quality of education imparted here.

Mission

  • To impart high-quality technical education across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels.
  • To foster a culture of **innovation and interdisciplinary research** addressing global challenges.
  • To instill strong ethical values and leadership qualities in students, preparing them to be socially responsible professionals.
  • To collaborate actively with industry and government bodies for technology transfer and applied research.

Vision

"To be recognized globally as a leading institution for engineering and technology, driving sustainable development through educational excellence, pioneering research, and dedicated service to humanity."

Admissions 2025-2026

Welcome to the Birsa Institute of Technology Admissions Portal. We offer rigorous programs based on national-level competitive examinations. Please review the criteria and schedule below.

B.Tech (Undergraduate)

Eligibility

  • 10+2 with minimum 75% aggregate in PCM.
  • Must have secured a rank in **JEE Main 2025**.
  • Age limit applies (see official brochure).

Selection Process

Admission is strictly based on the **JoSAA/CSAB counselling** process, utilizing the candidate's JEE Main All India Rank (AIR).

View JoSAA Schedule →

Important Dates

Application Window: **May 15 - June 30, 2025**
Counselling Begins: **July 2025**
Classes Start: **August 15, 2025**

M.Tech (Postgraduate)

Eligibility

B.E./B.Tech degree in the relevant discipline with minimum 60% aggregate or 6.5 CGPA.

  • Must possess a valid **GATE Score**.

Selection Process

Admission is handled through the **CCMT counselling** based on GATE score, followed by potential departmental interviews for certain specializations.
